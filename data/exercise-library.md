## Notes for AI Coach

- **Flat feet:** Prioritize calf raises, single-leg work, figure-4 stretch. Avoid high-impact jumping without proper footwear.
- **Lower back pain:** Start with glute bridges, bird dog, dead bug. Progress slowly to RDLs. Avoid good mornings until back is strong.
- **Max load:** 20kg total (barbell) or 2×10kg dumbbells.
- **Pull-up progression:** Dead hang → Band-assisted → Chin-up → Pull-up → Weighted
